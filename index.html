            What these numbers mean
            
      Headline totals for the whole file. Resolved counts tickets in a closed state (Closed, Resolved, or Ready to Close). Median times sit next to the averages because a handful of very long tickets can pull an average upward, so the median is usually the more honest "typical" figure. Avg interactions is the mean of Freshdesk's Customer interactions count — roughly how many replies/messages were exchanged on a ticket, so it's a proxy for how much back-and-forth each one needed (higher = more effort per ticket).
      
          
      

          
          
      
            
      
              
      Trends over time
      
              
      
                
                
              
      
            
      
            
      
              What this shows
              
      Six key metrics tracked across each week or month so you can spot the direction of travel at a glance. Created is bucketed by the ticket's created date; Resolved by its resolved/closed date; Avg first response, Avg resolution, First response SLA, and Resolution SLA are for the tickets created in each period (a cohort view). Each mini-chart is scaled to its own range; the big number is the latest period and the arrow shows the change from the one before, coloured green when it's moving the right way (faster responses, higher SLA, more resolved) and red when it isn't. The most recent period is often partial, so read the very last point with that in mind.
      
            
      
            
      
          
      

          
          
      
            
      Period-over-period
      
            
      
              What this shows
              
      The current week or month compared directly against the one before it, for the same six metrics as Trends above (uses whichever grouping is selected there). Useful for a quick "are we up or down vs last period" check without reading a chart. The current period is often partial (still in progress), so a dip there isn\u2019t necessarily a real decline \u2014 compare once the period has fully closed for the cleanest read.
      
            
      
            
      
          
      

          
          
      
            
      
              
      Ticket volume (created)
      
              
      
                
                
              
      
            
      
            
      
              What this shows
              
      How many tickets were created in each period, by the ticket's created date. Toggle Per day / Per week to smooth out day-to-day noise and see the broader trend. Weeks start on Monday; hover a bar for the exact count.
      
            
      
            
      
          
      

          
          
      
            
      Agent performance
      
            
      
              What this shows
              
      One row per agent. Tickets is the total assigned to them; Resolved is how many reached a closed state. Median first-response and resolution times reflect speed, and avg interactions reflects how much back-and-forth their tickets needed. Compare median resolution across agents rather than the average, since one stuck ticket can distort a mean.
      
            
      
            
      
          
      

          
          
      
            
      
              
      First-response time
      
              
      
                What this shows
                
      How long the first reply took, grouped into time bands. Weighted toward the left (under a few hours) is healthy — a large "> 24h" bar points to tickets waiting too long for an initial touch.
      
              
      
              
      
            
      
            
      
              
      Resolution time
      
              
      
                What this shows
                
      How long tickets took to fully resolve, in bands. A long tail (over 3 days) is worth digging into — it's often a few complex or escalated tickets.
      
              
      
              
      
            
      
          
      

          
          
      
            
      Customer interactions
      
            
      
              What this shows
              
      Customer interactions is Freshdesk's count of the back-and-forth messages exchanged with the requester on a ticket — each customer reply/conversation adds one. It's a proxy for how much effort a ticket took: a ticket resolved in a single interaction ("one-touch") was answered without further back-and-forth, while higher counts mean more rounds of clarification. The distribution below shows how many tickets needed each number of interactions; the one-touch rate is a common help-desk efficiency measure (higher is generally better, though very complex issues will naturally need more).
      
            
      
            
      
          
      

          
          
      
            
      Customer satisfaction — survey by priority
      
            
      
              What this shows
              
      Survey ratings on the 5-point scale (Excellent → Below Expectations), broken down by ticket priority. Only tickets the customer actually rated are counted, so each row also shows the response rate. The average score maps Excellent = 5 down to Below Expectations = 1.
      
            
      
            
      
          
      

          
          
      
            
      Escalated to JIRA
      
            
      
              What this shows
              
      Tickets tagged jiraescalated — i.e. handed to the DevTeam as a JIRA issue — grouped by their current Freshdesk status. This is a quick read on how many escalations are still open versus resolved/closed. Click a status to see those tickets (the drill-down table links straight to the JIRA issue where one is tagged).
      
            
      
            
      
          
      

          
          
      
            
      Re-opened
      
            
      
              What this shows
              
      Tickets tagged reopen — cases that were re-opened after being resolved/closed — grouped by their current Freshdesk status. A high or rising count can signal fixes that didn\u2019t fully land or answers that missed the mark. Click a status to see those tickets.
      
            
      
            
      
          
      

          
          
      
            
      Oldest unresolved tickets
      
            
      
              What this shows
              
      The 3 open tickets (not Closed or Resolved) that have been sitting the longest since they were created \u2014 the ones most overdue for a look. Click a ticket to jump straight to it, or see the full sorted list.

            What these numbers mean
            
      Totals reflect the currently filtered calls. Answered is Twilio status Completed (the call connected and finished); No answer, Busy, and Failed are the ways a call didn't connect. Talk time sums the duration of answered calls, and cost is the absolute total of the Price column (Twilio reports charges as negative amounts).
